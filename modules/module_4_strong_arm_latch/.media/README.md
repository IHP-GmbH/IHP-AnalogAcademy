# StrongARM Workshop Figures

These figures are copied from the `fig/` directory of the supplied `mixdes-workshop` material by Krzysztof Herman. They accompany the adapted Module 4 tutorials:

- `schem-basic.png` and `schem-final.png`: architectural and layout-compatible schematic views.
- `CMP7345_tb.png` and `CMP7345_tb_out.png`: nominal transient testbench and example waveforms.
- `CMP7345_tb_kickback_res.png`: example input-kickback waveforms.
- `layout-final.png` and `layout_diff_pair.png`: comparator and matched differential-pair layouts.

The figures illustrate the workshop design. Use the pinned `IHP__CMP7345` submodule and the conditions recorded in your own simulations when reporting results.
